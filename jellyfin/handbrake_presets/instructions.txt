Step 1: MakeMKV (The Rip) - installed with flatpak

Insert the DVD and open MakeMKV.

Click the large optical drive icon to scan the disc.

Check the box for the longest title (the main movie). Optional: Check the smaller titles if you want the trailers and special features.
Basically, check everything.

Expand the main movie title's tree and ensure all the audio and subtitle languages you want are checked.

Choose your output folder and click the Make MKV button.

Step 2: HandBrake (The Compression) - installed with flatpak

Open HandBrake and load the raw .mkv file you just created (if more, load one at a time).

Go to the Presets menu (usually a dropdown or a sidebar on the right) and select your custom preset (maybe you have to import it first).

Check the Audio and Subtitles tabs just to verify your tracks populated correctly.

Under "Save As" at the bottom, name your file using the English title and year: Movie Name (Year).mkv.

Click Start Encode.

Step 3: Organize and Transfer

On your local machine, put the finished .mkv file inside a folder named exactly the same: Movie Name (Year). (Add your trailers or extras folders inside here if you ripped them).

Open your terminal and send it across your secure virtual network using this command:

Bash
rsync -avzP "/path/to/local/Movie Name (Year)" username@your_netbird_ip:"/path/to/jellyfin/movies/"
Open your Jellyfin web dashboard, click Scan Library
